## AI Voice & Audio Workflow

My approach to AI voice production begins with the same principle I have used throughout my career in recording:

**Capture the performance first. Enhance it when necessary.**

A technically clean recording is not necessarily a compelling performance. Voice evaluation requires critical listening at both the technical and human levels — including phrasing, timing, articulation, emotion, timbre, consistency, breath, dynamics, and intent.

A typical workflow may include:

**Capture → Evaluate → Edit → Clean → Comp → Process → Quality Control → Label → Metadata → Delivery**

For AI voice datasets, consistency and organization are part of the engineering process. Files must be technically correct, clearly labeled, reproducible, and prepared so that downstream teams can work efficiently.

## Human + AI Production

I view AI as an extension of the creative production environment rather than a replacement for musicianship or engineering judgment.

My workflow combines traditional recording techniques, critical listening, DAW production, and generative systems while maintaining human creative direction throughout the process.

This includes moving material between generative environments and professional DAWs for editing, arrangement, overdubbing, processing, mixing, evaluation, and final delivery.
